        Company Stock Distribution Analysis Calculator

        Compare the taxes on taking company stock out of your 401(k) in shares, using net unrealized appreciation (NUA), with rolling it all over to an IRA. NUA means ordinary income tax on the cost basis now and long-term capital gains tax on the rest when you sell. The pivot point calculator online uses the same plain-English approach, so you can compare results side by side.

        NUA treatment needs a lump-sum distribution: your whole balance from the employer’s plans of that kind, paid within one tax year, after you leave the employer, reach 59½, become disabled (self-employed) or die. The rest of the plan can still be rolled over to an IRA.

        “Today’s dollars” discounts each year’s tax at the stock’s growth rate, because tax paid now costs you the growth on that money. Uses 2026 federal brackets for every year and ignores dividends.
