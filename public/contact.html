        Contact us

        Found a mistake, want a new calculator or have a question? We'd like to hear from you.

        The quickest way to reach us is by email.

        Email

        support@company-marketcap.github.io

        We read every message, and we aim to reply within a few business days.

        Reporting a calculator error

        If a result looks wrong, please include:

        • the name or web address of the calculator
        • the numbers you entered
        • the result you got, and the result you expected

        Those details let us reproduce the problem and fix it quickly.

        Suggesting a calculator

        Tell us what you're trying to work out. Many calculators on our financial calculators page are marked as coming soon, and suggestions help us decide which to build first.

        Please don't send personal financial details

        We can't give personal financial, tax or legal advice, so please don't email account numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive information.
