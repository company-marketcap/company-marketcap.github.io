        VA funding fee rates for 2026

        A percentage of the loan amount, the same for Veterans, active-duty service members and National Guard or Reserve members. Rates apply to loans closed on or after April 7, 2023, as published by the Department of Veterans Affairs.

        Loan typeDown paymentFirst useAfter first use
        Purchase or constructionLess than 5%2.15%3.3%
        Purchase or construction5% to less than 10%1.5%1.5%
        Purchase or construction10% or more1.25%1.25%

        Manufactured home (not permanently affixed)Any1%1%

        Who does not pay the fee

        You are exempt if you receive VA compensation for a service-connected disability, are eligible for that compensation but receive retirement or active-duty pay instead, are a surviving spouse receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, have a proposed or memorandum disability rating from a pre-discharge claim, or are on active duty and show evidence of a Purple Heart before closing.
