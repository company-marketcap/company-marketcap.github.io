        Which fees are finance charges

        Sorted under Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.4. Only finance charges feed the APR.

        FeeAmountFinance charge?Rule

        Title, document, notary, credit report and appraisal fees stay out of the APR only if they are bona fide and reasonable. A third-party fee becomes a finance charge when the lender requires the service and keeps part of the fee, and closing-agent fees count when the lender requires that particular service or charge (12 CFR 1026.4(a)(1)–(2)).
